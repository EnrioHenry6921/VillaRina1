Crikvenica

Forest on one side, the Adriatic on the other

Villa Rina sits in a quiet green pocket above Crikvenica on the Kvarner Riviera. Hiking trails begin at the doorstep, including the famous Path of Love with its ancient Roman ruins, while beaches, restaurants and the relaxed rhythm of the town wait a few minutes down the hill.
