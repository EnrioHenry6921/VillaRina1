About your hosts

A dream home, shared

The short story of why Villa Rina exists, and the two people behind it.

Welcome

We are Sandra and Robert Škiljan, and Villa Rina is our home. Years of travelling taught us how most holiday accommodation feels: like accommodation. A place to sleep between the good parts. We wanted to create the opposite, a house that is itself one of the good parts.

So when we built Villa Rina, we spared no expense and skipped no detail. It was conceived as a dream home first and a holiday rental second, which is exactly why it works so beautifully as both. We keep improving it every year, because we believe your holiday does not start at the beach. It starts and ends at your accommodation.

When you stay with us, you deal directly with the owners before, during and after your trip. We are nearby whenever you need something, and invisible when you do not.
