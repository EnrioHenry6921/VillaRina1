The Villa

Designed to the last detail

Two floors, 235 m², built in 2013 — modern architecture wrapped in forest, looking out over the sea.

4 bedrooms
3 bathrooms
8 guests, 4 beds
235 m² of living space
Heated floor-level pool with outdoor shower

Ground floor for living, first floor for sleeping — and the terrace for everything in between.

An open-plan heart

The ground floor is one generous space: a living room with a large corner sofa, a dining area and a fully equipped modern kitchen with an island.

Floor-to-ceiling sliding doors open straight onto the covered terrace, so inside and outside blur into one.

  • Corner sofa
  • Dining for 8
  • Kitchen island

A terrace made for whole days

Guests spend most of their time out here — barbecuing under the pergola, lounging on the daybeds, cooling off in the floor-level heated pool with its outdoor shower.

And always in front of you: the open Adriatic.

  • Heated pool
  • Pergola & BBQ
  • Sea view

Sleeps eight, beautifully

Four bedrooms on the first floor — calm, modern rooms, several with sea views and direct access to the rear terrace.

The bunk room makes the villa a favourite with families; kids claim it on sight.

  • 4 bedrooms
  • Sea views
  • Kids’ bunk room

Three modern bathrooms

A sculpted tub set against wave-relief tiles, a walk-in rain shower, warm wood tones — each bathroom has its own character, and all three are spotless.

Towels, bed linen and a hairdryer are, of course, provided.

  • Bathtub
  • Walk-in shower
  • Towels & linen
